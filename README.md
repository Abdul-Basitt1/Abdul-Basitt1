## About Me

<img align="right" alt="Coding GIF" width="285" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/qgQUggAC3Pfv687qPC/giphy.gif" />

I'm **Abdul Basit**, a **Mobile App Developer** working primarily with **React Native** and **Expo**.

I enjoy the entire process of building a mobile app, from turning designs into working interfaces to making sure everything runs smoothly on real devices.

My experience covers different types of mobile products, including **real-time applications, AI-powered features, crypto wallets, and payment integrations**.

**What I work on:**

- **Mobile development:** Cross-platform apps, responsive interfaces, navigation, and reusable components.
- **Real-time features:** Chat, Firebase, push notifications, live updates, and API integrations.
- **Web3 & blockchain:** Wallet functionality, transactions, and decentralized application integrations.
- **App quality:** Performance optimization, debugging, testing, and production releases.

Outside of mobile development, I also enjoy exploring **Game Development** and **Data Science**.
